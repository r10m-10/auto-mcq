FAQ

Q1 Is this against my platform's terms?

Possibly. AutoMCQ automates clicks on test-prep platforms, and many of those platforms prohibit automation in their terms. Use it at your own discretion — your account and your school's rules are your responsibility.

Q2 Is it safe? Can it see my answers?

It never reads or sends question text, answers, or any other page content. It only clicks the option you chose. The only data stored is a random device ID in your browser and your credit balance on the server.

Q3 How do credits work?

A normal click costs 1 credit and fires after a 5-second delay. A fast click costs 4 credits and fires instantly. Watch a rewarded ad to earn 3 credits. Every change is recorded on a server-side ledger you can view.

Q4 Where is my data stored?

A random device ID lives in your browser's local storage; your device ID and credit balance live on the AutoMCQ server. No personal information is collected. See the privacy policy for the full picture.
